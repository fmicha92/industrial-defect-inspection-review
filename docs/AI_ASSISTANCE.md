# AI assistance disclosure

AI agents have assisted with literature organization, structured extraction, graph linking, and code generation. AI output is treated as a draft or screening aid rather than independent evidence.

Release practice should include:

- human verification of dataset properties, study eligibility, numerical outcomes, and citation metadata;
- source-local citations for literature-dependent manuscript claims;
- explicit separation of reported facts, author interpretation, and automated inference;
- manual review of generated synthesis-impact candidates before inclusion;
- preservation of prompts, scripts, versions, and correction history when they materially affect an artifact; and
- disclosure in the associated manuscript according to the target journal's current policy.

No model-memory claim should be promoted into the evidence base without source verification.
