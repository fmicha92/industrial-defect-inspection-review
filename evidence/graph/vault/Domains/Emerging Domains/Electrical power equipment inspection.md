# Electrical power equipment inspection

## Definition

Electrical power equipment inspection covers visual defect detection for equipment used in electric power operation. The DG-GAN paper uses insulation partitions, including scratches, pit points, ablation, and voltage breakdown, as the supported example.

## Why It Matters

Insulation partition defects can affect electric power production safety, so the setting provides a non-steel industrial inspection case for defect image generation and downstream object detection.
