# Nondestructive Testing

## Definition

Nondestructive testing is staged as an emerging domain for inspection methods that examine parts without damaging them. In the current vault, this appears as industrial CT inspection of cast aluminum parts.

## Why It Matters

The current paper uses simulated CT volumes and voxel labels, which differ from ordinary 2D surface inspection and may deserve a stable domain if more CT or nondestructive inspection papers are added.

## Used In These Papers

- [[2021 - Defect Detection in CT Scans of Cast Aluminum Parts]] trains a 3D segmentation model on simulated CT data and evaluates transfer to real CT scans.
