# Medical imaging

## Definition

Medical imaging covers image-analysis tasks on clinical imaging data such as chest X-rays. In the linked anomaly-detection paper, it appears as an out-of-domain test of self-supervised synthetic anomaly training rather than an industrial inspection setting.
