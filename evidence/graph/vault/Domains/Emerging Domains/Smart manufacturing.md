# Smart Manufacturing

## Definition

Smart manufacturing is staged as an emerging domain for inspection settings framed around intelligent, edge-cloud, or online manufacturing systems. The current vault evidence comes from textured surface inspection and industrial anomaly localization papers.

## Why It Matters

This domain links inspection algorithms to deployment constraints such as edge inference, online inspection, and real production equipment.
