# Aircraft Maintenance

## Definition

Aircraft maintenance is staged as an emerging domain for borescope inspection and aircraft engine defect detection. The current vault evidence comes from compressor blade defect segmentation in borescope video frames.

## Why It Matters

This domain has different imaging conditions and maintenance constraints from ordinary factory-line surface inspection, including motion blur, small defects, and limited inspection imagery.

## Used In These Papers

- [[2023 - A Deep-Learning-Based Approach for Aircraft Engine Defect Detection]] uses DCGAN, Pix2Pix deblurring, and U-Net segmentation for high-pressure compressor blade inspection.
