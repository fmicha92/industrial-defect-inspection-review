# General computer vision

## Definition

General computer vision covers non-industry-specific visual recognition, translation, and scene understanding settings.

## Why It Matters

It gives broad method papers a neutral domain route when their evidence is not tied to a specific industrial material, sensor, or deployment setting.

## Used In These Papers

- [[2017 - Unpaired Image-to-Image Translation using Cycle-Consistent Adversarial Networks]] uses Cityscapes and other general image translation settings rather than a specific industrial domain.
- [[2016 - Deep Residual Learning for Image Recognition]] evaluates residual networks on ImageNet, CIFAR-10, and COCO visual recognition tasks.

## Related Datasets

- [[Cityscapes]]: used for labels-to-photos and photos-to-labels evaluation in a general image-to-image translation paper.
- [[ImageNet]]: large-scale WordNet-organized image dataset used for general visual recognition and pretraining.
- [[CIFAR-10]]: used for residual-network depth experiments and optimizer comparisons.
- [[COCO]]: used for detection and segmentation evidence in the residual-network paper.
- [[UCI classification benchmarks]]: grouped tabular benchmark note for the Random Forests paper.

## Related Papers
