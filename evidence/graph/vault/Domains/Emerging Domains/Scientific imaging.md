# Scientific Imaging

## Definition

Scientific imaging refers to image data acquired from scientific instruments or scientific experimental settings. In the linked paper, examples include microCT slices of composite fibers and rocks, plus high-resolution scans of plant roots.

## Why It Matters

Scientific images often contain fine-scale structures, modality-specific artifacts, and domain semantics that general-purpose image generators and image-text encoders may not preserve.

## Used In These Papers

- [[2025 - Synthetic Scientific Image Generation with VAE GAN and Diffusion Model Architectures]] evaluates whether generative models can produce scientifically meaningful images rather than only visually plausible images.
