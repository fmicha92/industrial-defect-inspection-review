# Aerospace and Composite Manufacturing

## Definition

Aerospace and composite manufacturing is staged as an emerging domain for inspection of composite layup and related aerospace production processes. The current vault evidence is automated fiber placement inspection using laser line scan sensor images.

## Why It Matters

The paper evidence emphasizes scarce defect examples in composite manufacturing, making synthetic image generation and defect classification important retrieval paths.
