# Pharmaceutical Packaging Inspection

## Definition

Pharmaceutical packaging inspection covers automated quality inspection for drug packaging integrity, including heat-sealed plastic bags where subtle micro-leakage can threaten product safety.

## Why It Matters

The thermal-imaging sealing-defect paper treats manual squeezing inspection as too slow and subjective for full production-line inspection, motivating nondestructive automated inspection with temporal thermal data.

## Used In These Papers

- [[2026 - Thermal Imaging for Sealing Defect Detection in Pharmaceutical Bags Using a Temporal Fusion Network]] uses thermal imaging to classify normal and defective heat-sealed pharmaceutical plastic bags.
