# Construction Inspection

## Definition

Construction inspection covers visual inspection of infrastructure such as concrete bridges and related civil structures. In the Defect-GAN evidence, it appears through CODEBRIM concrete-bridge defect imagery with crack, spallation, efflorescence, exposed-bar, corrosion, and normal classes.

## Why It Matters

The domain is useful to keep separate from factory manufacturing inspection until more papers clarify whether it should become a stable folder.

## Used In These Papers

- [[2021 - Defect-GAN High-Fidelity Defect Synthesis for Automated Defect Inspection]] evaluates GAN-based defect synthesis for automated defect inspection including construction imagery.
