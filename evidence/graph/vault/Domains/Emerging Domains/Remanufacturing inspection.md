# Remanufacturing Inspection

## Definition

Remanufacturing inspection checks whether used components can be restored and reused. In [[2025 - A Dataset and Baseline for Deep Learning-Based Visual Quality Inspection in Remanufacturing]], the setting is image-based OK/nOK assessment of worn automotive gearbox components.

## Why It Matters

Inspection decisions in remanufacturing often involve used, worn, and visually variable parts rather than newly manufactured items. This makes [[Distribution shift]] important because future parts may differ by component type, wear pattern, and acquisition setting.

## Used In These Papers

- [[2025 - A Dataset and Baseline for Deep Learning-Based Visual Quality Inspection in Remanufacturing]] introduces a public gearbox component dataset and split protocols for visual inspection in remanufacturing.
