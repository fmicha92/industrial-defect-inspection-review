# Parking Lot Vehicle Monitoring

## Definition

Parking lot vehicle monitoring localizes vehicle instances in parking-lot imagery, including dense scenes captured from drone viewpoints.

## Why It Matters

Dense parking-lot scenes stress few-shot object detection because many valid vehicle instances may be unannotated in sparse support images.
