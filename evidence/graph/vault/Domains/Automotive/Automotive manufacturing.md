# Automotive manufacturing

## Definition

Automotive manufacturing appears in the current vault through adhesive bead quality inspection, automotive plastic parts inspection, turbocharger housing inspection, and gearbox component remanufacturing inspection. These notes focus on defect detection under scarce real defect data.

## Represented Papers

- [[2024 - Open Stamped Parts Dataset]] introduces [[Open Stamped Parts Dataset]] for stamped metal-sheet hole detection in auto manufacturing.
- [[2024 - Benchmark for Welding Gun Fault Prediction with Multivariate Time Series Data]] introduces [[RSW gun fault prediction benchmark dataset]] for resistance spot welding gun fault prediction in automotive body-shop production.
- [[2021 - Simulation-Based Data Augmentation for Quality Inspection of Structural Adhesive]] studies adhesive bead defect detection in an automotive production cell.
- [[2021 - Generative Adversarial Networks for Data Augmentation in Structural Adhesive Inspection]] studies GAN-based augmentation for adhesive bead defect detection in an automotive pilot cell.
- [[2023 - Collaborative Discrepancy Optimization for Reliable Image Anomaly Localization]] evaluates on an automotive plastic parts inspection dataset.
- [[2022 - Procedural Synthetic Training Data Generation for AI-Based Defect Detection]] demonstrates synthetic data generation on turbocharger housing cavities.
- [[2025 - A Dataset and Baseline for Deep Learning-Based Visual Quality Inspection in Remanufacturing]] studies OK/nOK classification for used automotive gearbox components.
