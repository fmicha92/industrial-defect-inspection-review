# Attention-Conditioned Augmentations for Self-Supervised Anomaly Detection and Localization

## Summary

The paper combines attention-conditioned masked image modeling with attention-guided synthetic anomaly fine-tuning. On [[MVTec AD]], it reports 98.3% image-level AUROC and 98.2% pixel-wise AUROC, with ablations showing that attention-conditioned synthetic anomalies outperform CutPaste, NSA, and FPI under the same fine-tuning setup.

## Paper Type

- Type: research.
- Why: It proposes a new self-supervised pretraining and synthetic anomaly fine-tuning method and evaluates it against prior anomaly detection/localization baselines.

## Problem

- Task: [[Industrial anomaly detection]] and [[Defect segmentation]].
- Setting: normal-only training on industrial categories.
- Inputs: RGB industrial images from [[MVTec AD]].
- Outputs: image-level anomaly predictions and pixel-level anomaly maps.
- Motivation: random patch masking and random synthetic anomaly locations can ignore salient object regions.
- Assumptions: transformer self-attention can identify salient regions useful for both pretraining masks and synthetic anomaly placement.

## Contribution

- Claimed: Introduces attention-conditioned patch masking, APMask, for ViT-based self-supervised pretraining.
- Claimed: Uses self-attention maps to place synthetic pasted-patch anomalies in salient regions.
- Shown: The proposed method improves MVTec AD fine-tuning over synthetic anomaly baselines, including CutPaste, NSA, and FPI.

## Method

- Core idea: use ViT self-attention maps to drop least-attended patches during pretraining and to guide synthetic anomaly creation during fine-tuning.
- Architecture / algorithm: ViT-S/16 encoder initialized from DINO weights; MLP projection head; prototypes for clustering assignments.
- Objective / loss: consistency-based masked image modeling pretraining; fine-tuning combines image-level detection and segmentation losses.
- Optimization: AdamW; 300 pretraining epochs and 100 fine-tuning epochs in the extracted setup.
- Training data: normal MVTec AD training images.
- Data pipeline: local views are generated by random region cropping/resizing; synthetic anomalies are made by smoothly blending patches at attention-selected salient regions.
- Augmentation / synthesis: attention-guided synthetic pasted patches plus multi-scale test-time augmentation.
- Inference: multi-scale evaluation averages segmentation maps and uses majority voting over scale-transformed image-level predictions.

## Evidence

### Experimental Setup

- Datasets: [[MVTec AD]]; NIH chest X-ray is also used, but the industrial result is MVTec AD.
- Splits: source states MVTec AD has 3,629 defect-free training images and 1,725 test images with pixel-level annotations.
- Baselines: CutPaste, NSA, DRAEM, PaDiM, DifferNet, InTra, SPADE, RIAD, DINO, iBOT, MSN, FPI.
- Used performance metrics: image-level AUROC and pixel-wise AUROC.
- Hardware: single GeForce RTX 2080 Ti.

### Ablations

- Fine-tuning with attention-guided synthetic anomalies outperforms CutPaste, NSA, and FPI under the same backbone.
- APMask improves over random masking and no masking.
- Masking ratio 0.3 gives the best reported APMask setting among 0.15, 0.3, and 0.5.

## ML / DL Extraction

### Data

- Datasets: [[MVTec AD]].
- Labels / annotations: normal training images and pixel-level anomaly annotations for defective test images.
- Preprocessing: ViT local/global crops; exact image resizing for MVTec beyond view sizes is not fully reported.
- Augmentation: DINO-style view augmentations, attention-conditioned patch masking, synthetic patch blending, and multi-scale test-time augmentation.
- Synthetic data: pasted patches guided by ViT attention maps.
- Leakage checks: not reported.

### Evaluation

- Protocol: category-level MVTec AD anomaly detection and localization.
- Baselines: listed above.
- Statistical tests: ablation table reports standard error across five random seeds for some comparisons.
- Failure cases: the source notes low-resolution attention maps can limit fine-grained localization, motivating multi-scale evaluation.

## Limitations

- Stated: fine-grained local anomalies can be difficult with relatively low-resolution attention maps.
- Inferred: performance depends on the ViT attention map identifying object-salient regions reliably.

## Reproducibility

- Code: not reported.
- Data: MVTec AD is public.
- Hyperparameters: masking ratio 0.3, temperature 0.1, 20 prototypes, batch size 16, AdamW, learning-rate warmup to 5e-4.
- Random seeds: five seeds in ablation comparisons.
- Environment: PyTorch 1.9 and GeForce RTX 2080 Ti.
