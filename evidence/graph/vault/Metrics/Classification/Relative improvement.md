# Relative Improvement

## Definition

Relative improvement reports percentage improvement over a baseline. The SDGAN paper uses IMP to compare augmented recognition models with a CNN-only baseline.

## Direction

Higher is better.

## Used In These Papers

- [[2020 - Defect Image Sample Generation With GAN for Improving Defect Recognition]] reports 49.43% IMP for anomaly recognition on CCSD-NL and 57.47% IMP for defect classification on CCSD-L.
