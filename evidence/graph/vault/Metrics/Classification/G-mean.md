# G-Mean

## Definition

G-mean is an imbalance-aware classification metric based on the geometric mean of class-wise performance terms. The exact formula is not reported in the wafer-map paper's extracted text.

## Direction

Higher is better.

## Used In These Papers

- [[2020 - A Light-Weight Neural Network for Wafer Map Classification Based on Data Augmentation]] reports 81.71% G-mean for DMC1 on [[WM-811K]].
