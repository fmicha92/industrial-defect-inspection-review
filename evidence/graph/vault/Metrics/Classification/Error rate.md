# Error Rate

## Definition

Error rate is the percentage of recognition examples classified incorrectly. In defect recognition papers, lower error rate indicates better anomaly recognition or defect classification.

## Direction

Lower is better.

## Used In These Papers

- [[2020 - Defect Image Sample Generation With GAN for Improving Defect Recognition]] reports SDGAN-augmented VGG16 error rates of 1.77% on CCSD-NL anomaly recognition and 0.74% on CCSD-L defect classification.
- [[2011 - Non-parametric Texture Defect Detection Using Weibull Features]] reports equal error rates of 8.5%, 0.1%, 1.3%, and 3.2% on four [[DAGM2007]] texture classes.
