# Specificity

## Definition

Specificity measures the share of true negatives that are correctly identified. In defect detection, it measures how accurately defect-free samples are classified as defect-free.

## Direction

Higher is better.

## Used In These Papers

- [[2019 - A Public Fabric Database for Defect Detection Methods and Results]] reports specificity values of 136/140 = 97.14% for test 1 and 124/140 = 88.57% for test 2.
- [[2022 - Procedural Synthetic Training Data Generation for AI-Based Defect Detection]] reports specificity for turbocharger housing blowhole classification.
- [[2022 - Synthetic Data Augmentation for Surface Defect Detection and Classification Using Deep Learning]] reports specificity for NEU-CLS steel surface defect classification, including 99.16% mean specificity for DCGAN augmentation.
- [[2026 - IncepViTH-Net for Solar Panel Fault Detection and Classification]] reports TNR as 96.577% at K value 8 for solar panel fault classification.
