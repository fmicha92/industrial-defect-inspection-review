# Fill Factor

## Definition

Fill factor is a photovoltaic performance metric describing the squareness of a solar-cell current-voltage curve.

## Direction

Higher is better.

## Used In These Papers

- [[2021 - Comprehensive Device Modelling and Performance Analysis of MASnI3 Based Perovskite Solar Cells]] reports a best-configuration FF of 69.23%.
