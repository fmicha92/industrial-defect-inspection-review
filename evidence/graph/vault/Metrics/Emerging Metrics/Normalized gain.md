# Normalized gain

## Definition

Normalized gain measures pre/post assessment improvement as the ratio of actual gain to maximum possible gain. In [[1998 - Interactive-engagement versus Traditional Methods]], course-level average normalized gain is defined as `(%<post> - %<pre>) / (100 - %<pre>)`.

## Why It Matters

The metric allowed Hake to compare mechanics concept-test gains across courses with different average pre-test scores. It became a foundation for later physics education measurement work, including [[2007 - Normalized Change]], which critiques its behavior for score declines, asymmetry, and ceiling cases.

## Used In These Papers

- [[1998 - Interactive-engagement versus Traditional Methods]] uses average normalized gain as the primary statistic for comparing traditional and interactive-engagement introductory mechanics courses.
- [[2007 - Normalized Change]] uses normalized gain as the baseline statistic that normalized change modifies.
