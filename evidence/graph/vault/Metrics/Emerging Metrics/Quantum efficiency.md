# Quantum Efficiency

## Definition

Quantum efficiency describes the spectral photon-to-electron conversion response of a photovoltaic device.

## Direction

Higher is better across the useful spectral range.

## Used In These Papers

- [[2021 - Comprehensive Device Modelling and Performance Analysis of MASnI3 Based Perovskite Solar Cells]] reports QE remaining constant to 700 nm and dropping to zero at 960 nm for the optimized configuration.
