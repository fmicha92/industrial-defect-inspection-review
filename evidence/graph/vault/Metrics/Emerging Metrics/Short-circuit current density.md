# Short-Circuit Current Density

## Definition

Short-circuit current density is the current density generated by a photovoltaic device under short-circuit conditions.

## Direction

Higher is generally better when comparing solar-cell output under the same conditions.

## Used In These Papers

- [[2021 - Comprehensive Device Modelling and Performance Analysis of MASnI3 Based Perovskite Solar Cells]] reports JSC of 34.26863 mA/cm2 for the best configuration.
