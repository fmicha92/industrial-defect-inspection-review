# D1-all

## Definition

D1-all is the KITTI stereo-disparity outlier/error metric reported by Mayer et al. for KITTI 2015 disparity evaluation.

## Used In These Papers

- [[2016 - A Large Dataset to Train Convolutional Networks for Disparity Optical Flow and Scene Flow Estimation]] reports KITTI 2015 D1-all results for DispNet variants and stereo baselines.
