# Power Conversion Efficiency

## Definition

Power conversion efficiency measures the percentage of incident solar energy converted to electrical energy by a photovoltaic device.

## Direction

Higher is better.

## Used In These Papers

- [[2021 - Comprehensive Device Modelling and Performance Analysis of MASnI3 Based Perovskite Solar Cells]] reports a best simulated PCE of 25.05% for Glass/FTO/PCBM/MASnI3/CuI/Au.
