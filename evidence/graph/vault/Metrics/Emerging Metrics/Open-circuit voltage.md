# Open-Circuit Voltage

## Definition

Open-circuit voltage is the voltage across a photovoltaic device when no external current flows.

## Direction

Higher is generally better when comparing solar-cell output under the same conditions.

## Used In These Papers

- [[2021 - Comprehensive Device Modelling and Performance Analysis of MASnI3 Based Perovskite Solar Cells]] reports VOC of 1.0557 V for the best configuration.
- [[2025 - Advanced Photovoltaic Module Characterization Using Image Transformers for Current-Voltage Curve Prediction From Electroluminescence Images]] evaluates Voc derived from predicted I-V curves and reports mean error below 1%.
