# Hand pose threshold accuracy

## Definition

Hand pose threshold accuracy reports the percentage of images whose predicted hand keypoints fall within a specified pixel distance from ground truth.

## Direction

Higher is better.

## Used In These Papers

- [[2017 - Learning from Simulated and Unsupervised Images through Adversarial Training]] reports the percentage of hand-pose images within 5 pixels of ground truth on the NYU hand pose dataset.
