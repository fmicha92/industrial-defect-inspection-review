# Grasp Success Rate

## Definition

Grasp success rate is the fraction of attempted robotic pickups that succeed.

## Used In These Papers

- [[2017 - Domain Randomization for Transferring Deep Neural Networks from Simulation to the Real World]] reports 38 of 40 and 9 of 10 pickup successes in its robotic experiments.
