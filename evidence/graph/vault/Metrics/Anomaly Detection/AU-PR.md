# AU-PR

## Definition

Area under the precision-recall curve; higher values indicate better precision-recall tradeoff.

## Why It Matters

This item supports industrial defect detection, anomaly detection, classification, segmentation, generation, or benchmark evaluation.

## Used In These Papers

- [[2020 - Self-Supervised Representation Learning for Wafer Bin Map Defect Pattern Classification]] reports AUPRC for WaPIRL downstream wafer-bin-map classification comparisons.

- [[2022 - SPot-the-Difference Self-Supervised Pre-training for Anomaly Detection and Segmentation]] emphasizes AU-PR for imbalanced anomaly detection and segmentation, reporting SPD gains such as +6.8 points for supervised 2-class high-shot VisA segmentation.
- [[2024 - A Comprehensive Augmentation Framework for Anomaly Detection]] reports AP alongside AUROC for MVTec AD detection and localization, including 70.9 localization AP for the full method.
- [[2024 - Industrial Product Surface Anomaly Detection with Realistic Synthetic Anomalies Based on Defect Map Prediction]] reports AP for MVTec AD localization and ablates the full method at 99.87 AP.
