# Global Accuracy

## Definition

Global accuracy measures the overall proportion of correctly classified pixels in semantic segmentation.

- [[2016 - The SYNTHIA Dataset A Large Collection of Synthetic Images for Semantic Segmentation of Urban Scenes]] reports global accuracy alongside per-class accuracy.
