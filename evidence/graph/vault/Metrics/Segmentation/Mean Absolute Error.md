# Mean Absolute Error

## Definition

Mean Absolute Error measures the average absolute difference between predicted saliency or segmentation output and the corresponding ground-truth mask.

## Direction

Lower is better.

## Used In These Papers

- [[2020 - Surface Defect Saliency of Magnetic Tile]] reports MAE for magnetic-tile surface-defect saliency maps on [[Magnetic Tile Defects]].
