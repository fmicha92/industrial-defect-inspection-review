# Inception Score

## Definition

Inception Score is recorded as a generative-quality metric when linked papers report it. Exact model, preprocessing, and sample-count settings are `not reported` unless stated by the source paper.

## Used In These Papers

- [[2025 - Free Lunch of Image-mask Alignment for Anomaly Image Generation and Segmentation]] reports Inception Score 3.25 for generated polyp images, compared with ArSDM at 1.98.

- [[2023 - Intraclass Image Augmentation for Defect Detection Using Generative Adversarial Neural Networks]] reports IS for generated defect images.
- [[2024 - AnomalyXFusion Multi-modal Anomaly Synthesis with Diffusion]] reports mean IS 1.82 on MVTec AD and 1.69 on MVTec LOCO for anomaly synthesis.
- [[2020 - Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Models]] reports CIFAR-10 Inception Score 9.46 +/- 0.11 for its simplified-objective diffusion model.
