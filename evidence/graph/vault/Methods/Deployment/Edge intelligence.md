# Edge intelligence

## Definition

Edge intelligence refers to running inspection intelligence close to the production equipment rather than only in centralized cloud systems. In the paper notes, it appears in the FMR-Net paper through edge-cloud manufacturing and NVIDIA Jetson AGX edge nodes.

## Why It Matters

Industrial inspection can have latency and deployment constraints. Edge intelligence matters when models must support online inspection, early exits, or multilevel inference near the inspection hardware.
