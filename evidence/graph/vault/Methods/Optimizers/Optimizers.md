# Optimizers

## Definition

Optimizers are training algorithms that update model parameters from gradients or other search signals during model fitting.

## Scope

Use this folder for reusable optimization algorithms and optimizer-family notes, including SGD, Adam, AdamW, RMSProp, momentum variants, learning-rate schedules when treated as standalone training methods, and optimizer-specific procedures.
