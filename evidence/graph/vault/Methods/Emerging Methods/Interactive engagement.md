# Interactive engagement

## Definition

Interactive engagement methods are instructional methods designed to promote conceptual understanding through heads-on, and usually hands-on, student activity with immediate feedback through peers or instructors. [[1998 - Interactive-engagement versus Traditional Methods]] contrasts these methods with traditional passive-student lectures, recipe labs, and algorithmic-problem exams.

## Why It Matters

Hake's survey reports that interactive-engagement courses had substantially higher average normalized gains on mechanics concept tests than traditional courses, while also showing higher Mechanics Baseline problem-solving scores in the available subset.

## Used In These Papers

- [[1998 - Interactive-engagement versus Traditional Methods]] compares 48 interactive-engagement courses with 14 traditional courses and reports average normalized gains of 0.48 +/- 0.14 versus 0.23 +/- 0.04.
