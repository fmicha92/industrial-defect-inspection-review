# One-Hot Multi-Label Wafer Defect Decoding

## Definition

One-hot multi-label wafer defect decoding represents a mixed wafer defect as membership over basic single defect patterns rather than as one monolithic mixed class.

## Why It Matters

It reduces the feature space for mixed-type wafer-map recognition and lets a network decompose a complex mixed pattern into its component defects.

## Used In These Papers

- [[2020 - Deformable Convolutional Networks for Efficient Mixed-Type Wafer Defect Pattern Recognition]] combines this output mechanism with DC-Net.
