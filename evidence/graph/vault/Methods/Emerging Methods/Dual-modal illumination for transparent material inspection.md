# Dual-Modal Illumination for Transparent Material Inspection

## Definition

Dual-modal illumination for transparent material inspection captures paired images of the same point under forward lighting and backward lighting.

## Why It Matters

The aircraft canopy paper reports that some defects are visible under one lighting direction but missed or confused under another. Forward lighting captures surface scratch-like defects well, while backward lighting can reveal depth or spot information in transparent material.
