# Total Reflection Grazing Incidence Combined Lighting

## Definition

Total reflection grazing incidence combined lighting is an optical inspection setup that pairs a main total-reflection light source with an auxiliary low-angle grazing light source for mobile phone flat-glass defect imaging.

## Why It Matters

The Ding and Yang paper uses the combined lighting to improve separation between bright spot defects, dust, scratches, and stains, which are difficult to distinguish under conventional illumination.

## Used In These Papers

- [[2024 - Glass Defect Detection with Improved Data Augmentation under Total Reflection Lighting]] designs a TR-OD light source as part of an automatic optical inspection platform for mobile phone flat glass.
