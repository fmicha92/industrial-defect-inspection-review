# DPG-DT

## Definition

DPG-DT is a differentially private generative digital-twin framework for synthesizing industrial images under class imbalance. Its PSIE component uses energy-based generation with privacy mechanisms rather than relying only on conventional GAN generation.

## Why It Matters

It connects synthetic data generation with privacy constraints for industrial IoT settings where defect data can be scarce or sensitive.

## Used In These Papers

- [[2023 - DPG-DT Differentially Private Generative Digital Twin for Imbalanced Learning in Industrial IoT]] introduces DPG-DT and reports up to 23.9% downstream classification improvement over private generative baselines in industrial scenarios.
