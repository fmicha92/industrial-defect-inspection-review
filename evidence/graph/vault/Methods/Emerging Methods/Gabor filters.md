# Gabor Filters

## Definition

Gabor filters are band-pass texture filters formed from a Gaussian envelope and a complex oscillation. They are localized in both spatial and frequency domains and can be arranged as a filter bank by varying parameters such as wavelength, orientation, phase offset, Gaussian deviation, and spatial aspect ratio.

## Why It Matters

For fabric defect detection, Gabor filters can highlight texture deviations and defect contours in otherwise regular textile textures.
