# SCAPS 1D

## Definition

SCAPS 1D is a solar-cell device simulation package used to model planar heterojunction photovoltaic devices through coupled Poisson and carrier-continuity equations.

## Why It Matters

It supports simulation of layer thicknesses, defect densities, dopant concentrations, contacts, and temperature effects before physical solar-cell fabrication.

- [[2021 - Comprehensive Device Modelling and Performance Analysis of MASnI3 Based Perovskite Solar Cells]] uses SCAPS 1D version 3.3.08 to simulate MASnI3 perovskite solar cells.
