# Contrastive GAN

## Definition

Contrastive GAN, or Con-GAN, is a GAN-based defect synthesis method that combines shared data augmentation, feature-alignment matching, and sphere contrastive loss to train under extremely limited defect samples.

## Why It Matters

It captures a reusable technical approach for handling scarce, imbalanced, or hard-to-label defect data in industrial visual inspection.
