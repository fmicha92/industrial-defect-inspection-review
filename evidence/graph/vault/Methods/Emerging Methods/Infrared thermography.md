# Infrared Thermography

## Definition

Infrared thermography captures thermal radiation patterns to inspect physical assets. In [[2020 - Infrared Solar Module Dataset for Anomaly Detection]], infrared imagery from aircraft and unmanned aerial systems is used to identify and classify solar-module anomalies.

## Why It Matters

Thermal inspection can reveal solar-module conditions such as hot spots, bypass-diode activation, shadowing, vegetation obstruction, soiling, and offline modules without direct contact with the PV system.

## Used In These Papers

- [[2020 - Infrared Solar Module Dataset for Anomaly Detection]] uses midwave or longwave infrared imagery as the core sensing modality for [[InfraredSolarModules]].
- [[2025 - Synthetic Dataset for AI-Driven Photovoltaic Fault and Damage Detection via UAV Thermal Imaging]] uses UAV-acquired longwave infrared imagery for PV panel and fault detection.
