# DE-SSD

## Definition

DE-SSD is a semi-supervised PCB defect detection method that combines a YOLOv5x detector, batch-adding semi-supervised learning, dynamic-threshold pseudo labels, and auxiliary negative samples from PKU-PCB.

## Why It Matters

The linked paper uses this method as a central mechanism for industrial defect detection, anomaly detection, anomaly localization, or data-efficient inspection.

## Used In These Papers

- [[2022 - Semi-Supervised Defect Detection Method with Data-Expanding Strategy for PCB Quality Inspection]] introduces DE-SSD and reports 98.4% mAP on DeepPCB with 50 labeled samples.
