# Graph Transformer Network

## Definition

A graph transformer network is a trainable system that transforms directed graphs with numerical arc values, enabling gradient-based training of structured document-recognition pipelines.

## Why It Matters

LeCun et al. use graph transformer networks to connect segmentation, recognition, and language constraints in document-recognition systems and train the combined system globally.

## Used In These Papers

- [[1998 - Gradient-Based Learning Applied to Document Recognition]] develops graph transformer networks for handwritten word recognition and check reading.
