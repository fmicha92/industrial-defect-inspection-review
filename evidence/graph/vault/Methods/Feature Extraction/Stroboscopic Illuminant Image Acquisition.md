# Stroboscopic Illuminant Image Acquisition

## Definition

Stroboscopic Illuminant Image Acquisition is the multi-angle lighting setup introduced in [[2025 - A Dataset for Surface Defect Detection on Complex Structured Parts Based on Photometric Stereo]]. It sequentially strobes illuminants around a metal part while cameras capture images under different lighting directions.

## Why It Matters

The method supports [[Photometric stereo]] inspection on non-planar casting parts, where static illumination can confuse stains, shadows, and geometric defects.

## Used In These Papers

- [[2025 - A Dataset for Surface Defect Detection on Complex Structured Parts Based on Photometric Stereo]] uses SIIA to collect [[MSDD]] images and reports acquisition within 0.96 seconds for a 300-pieces-per-hour casting line.
