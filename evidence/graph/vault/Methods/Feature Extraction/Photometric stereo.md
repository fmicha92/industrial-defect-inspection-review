# Photometric Stereo

## Definition

Photometric stereo is an imaging method that estimates surface shape cues from images captured under different lighting directions.

## Why It Matters

It supports defect inspection on structured or non-planar parts where surface shape, shadows, stains, and illumination angle can make ordinary 2D image inspection unreliable.
