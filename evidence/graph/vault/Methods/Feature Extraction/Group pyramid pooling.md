# Group Pyramid Pooling

## Definition

Group pyramid pooling is a feature extraction module proposed for online PCB defect detection.

## Why It Matters

The processed DeepPCB paper reports that GPP improves mAP while keeping the detector fast.

## Used In These Papers

- [[2019 - Online PCB Defect Detector on a New PCB Defect Dataset]] proposes group pyramid pooling for PCB defect detection.
