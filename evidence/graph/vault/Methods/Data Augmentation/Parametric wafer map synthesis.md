# Parametric Wafer Map Synthesis

## Definition

Parametric wafer map synthesis generates synthetic wafer defect patterns from mathematical descriptions of defect classes and regularization steps.

## Why It Matters

It is used to train wafer-map classifiers when only a small amount of experimental labeled data is available.

## Used In These Papers

- [[2019 - Classification of Wafer Maps Defect Based on Deep Learning Methods With Small Amount of Data]] creates a composite training dataset from synthetic wafer maps plus about 20 experimental examples.
