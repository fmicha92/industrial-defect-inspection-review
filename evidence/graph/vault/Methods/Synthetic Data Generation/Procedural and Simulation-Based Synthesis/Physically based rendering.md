# Physically based rendering

## Definition

Physically based rendering is a rendering approach intended to approximate realistic material and lighting behavior. In the paper notes, it is used through Blender/Cycles to generate synthetic industrial inspection images.

## Why It Matters

PBR matters because synthetic visual realism can affect whether a model trained on rendered data transfers to real inspection images. The local evidence links it to procedural textures, domain randomization, and synthetic pretraining.

## Used In These Papers

- [[2022 - Procedural Synthetic Training Data Generation for AI-Based Defect Detection]] uses Blender, Cycles, procedural textures, and physically based rendering to synthesize turbocharger housing defect images.
