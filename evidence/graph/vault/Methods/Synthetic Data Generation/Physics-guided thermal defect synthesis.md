# Physics-Guided Thermal Defect Synthesis

## Definition

Physics-guided thermal defect synthesis generates synthetic thermal defect samples by modeling defect contours, simulating thermal diffusion, and fusing the resulting defect masks into normal thermal images under geometric constraints.

## Why It Matters

The pharmaceutical-bag sealing paper uses this method to expand 28 real defective samples into 2104 synthetic defective samples without training a generative model.

## Used In These Papers

- [[2026 - Thermal Imaging for Sealing Defect Detection in Pharmaceutical Bags Using a Temporal Fusion Network]] uses hybrid polynomial contour modeling, dual-Gaussian thermal diffusion, and boundary-aware fusion to generate defective thermal samples.
