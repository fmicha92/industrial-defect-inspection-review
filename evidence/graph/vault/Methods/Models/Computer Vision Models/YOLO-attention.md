# YOLO-attention

## Definition

YOLO-attention is a YOLOv4-based object detector for online WAAM defect detection. It adds a DarkNet53-attention backbone, multiple SPP branches, and EMA training to improve small-target detection.

## Why It Matters

It is a paper-specific real-time detector for WAAM metallic surface defects, reporting 94.5 mAP@0.5 at 42 FPS on the authors' WAAM dataset.
