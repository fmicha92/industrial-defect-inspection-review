# InceptionV3

## Definition

InceptionV3 is a convolutional neural-network architecture used for image classification and transfer learning.

## Why It Matters

InceptionV3 is the architecture family used by DeepSolarDE and fine-tuned as DeepSolarDK for solar-panel mapping from aerial imagery.
