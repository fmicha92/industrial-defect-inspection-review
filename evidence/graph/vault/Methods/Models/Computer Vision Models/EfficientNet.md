# EfficientNet

## Definition

EfficientNet is a convolutional neural network family that scales depth, width, and input resolution together to balance accuracy and computational cost.

## Why It Matters

EfficientNet-style backbones provide comparatively compact image classifiers for industrial visual inspection settings where limited data and deployment constraints can make heavier CNNs less attractive.

## Used In These Papers

- [[2025 - Enhancing Glass Defect Detection with Diffusion Models]] evaluates EfficientNetB0 with and without DDPM-generated defective glass bottle images; recall improves from 0.35 to 0.65 while precision remains 1.00.
- [[2022 - SolarDK A high-resolution urban solar panel image classification and localization dataset]] evaluates EfficientNet-b5 and EfficientNet-b7 for binary PV/no-PV classification from Danish aerial imagery.
