# ResNet152V2

## Definition

ResNet152V2 is a deep residual convolutional neural network variant used for image classification. It belongs to the ResNet family and uses residual connections to support training of very deep networks.

## Why It Matters

In industrial visual inspection studies, ResNet152V2 can serve as a high-capacity classifier for assessing whether augmentation or synthetic data improves defect classification without changing the classifier architecture.
