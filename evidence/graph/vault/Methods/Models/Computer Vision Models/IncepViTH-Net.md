# IncepViTH-Net

## Definition

IncepViTH-Net is a hybrid computer-vision model for solar panel fault detection and classification. It combines an InceptionV3-style convolutional branch, a [[Vision Transformer]] branch, and harmonic analysis to fuse branch outputs.

## Why It Matters

The model is designed to classify photovoltaic panel surface states after image enhancement, [[U-Net]] region segmentation, and feature extraction. The source paper reports improved [[Accuracy]], [[Recall]], and [[Specificity]] over SPF-Net, PV-YOLO, Coupled UDenseNet, and RMVDM on a Kaggle subset represented by [[PV Panel Defect Dataset]].
