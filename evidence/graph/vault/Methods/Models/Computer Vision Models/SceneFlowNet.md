# SceneFlowNet

## Definition

SceneFlowNet is a convolutional network for jointly predicting optical flow, disparity, and disparity change.

## Why It Matters

It uses synthetic scene-flow supervision to train a single network for dense stereo-motion outputs.

## Used In These Papers

- [[2016 - A Large Dataset to Train Convolutional Networks for Disparity Optical Flow and Scene Flow Estimation]] trains SceneFlowNet on FlyingThings3D and evaluates it across Driving, FlyingThings3D, and Monkaa.
