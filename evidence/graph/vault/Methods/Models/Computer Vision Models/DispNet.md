# DispNet

## Definition

DispNet is a FlowNet-style convolutional encoder-decoder network adapted for stereo disparity estimation.

## Why It Matters

It demonstrates that synthetic stereo data can train fast disparity estimators that transfer to KITTI after fine-tuning.

## Used In These Papers

- [[2016 - A Large Dataset to Train Convolutional Networks for Disparity Optical Flow and Scene Flow Estimation]] trains and evaluates DispNet and DispNetCorr1D on synthetic and KITTI disparity tasks.
