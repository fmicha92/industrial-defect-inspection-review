# Single Shot MultiBox Detector

## Definition

Single Shot MultiBox Detector is an object-detection model family used as the basis for EDDN in the metallic surface defect paper.

## Why It Matters

It provides a one-stage detection framework for defect localization and classification.

## Used In These Papers

- [[2020 - Deep Metallic Surface Defect Detection The New Benchmark and Detection Network]] builds EDDN on SSD and compares against SSD, Faster R-CNN, YOLO-V2, and YOLO-V3.
- [[2022 - Video-Based Two-Stage Network for Optical Glass Sub-Millimeter Defect Detection]] compares against SSD300 and SSD512 on [[OGD-DET benchmark]].
