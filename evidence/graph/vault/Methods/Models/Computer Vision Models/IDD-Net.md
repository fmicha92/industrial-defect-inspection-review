# IDD-Net

## Definition

IDD-Net is a one-stage industrial defect detector proposed by [[2023 - IDD-Net Industrial Defect Detection Method Based on Deep-Learning]]. It combines [[LGB-Net]], [[TFLA-Net]], [[Multi-scale feature fusion]], and [[Defect-IoU loss]] to improve small and multi-scale defect detection.

## Why It Matters

It shows a YOLO-family detector modification evaluated across steel, aluminium, PCB, and glass-bottle production data, with both accuracy and speed metrics.

## Used In These Papers

- [[2023 - IDD-Net Industrial Defect Detection Method Based on Deep-Learning]] introduces IDD-Net and reports 99.5% [[mAP@0.50]] and 40 [[FPS]] on [[BBMP]].
