# RBCNN

## Definition

RBCNN is the reference-based PCB inspection plus convolutional neural network approach used in the HRIPCB paper: it first locates defects, then classifies cropped defect regions with a CNN.

## Why It Matters

It separates PCB defect localization from defect category classification on HRIPCB.

## Used In These Papers

- [[2020 - HRIPCB a Challenging Dataset for PCB Defects Detection and Classification]] proposes and evaluates RBCNN.
