# EDDN

## Definition

EDDN is an end-to-end defect detection network based on Single Shot MultiBox Detector, proposed for metallic surface defect detection.

## Why It Matters

The processed paper uses EDDN to handle defects at different scales, with hard negative mining for data imbalance and data augmentation for limited data.

## Used In These Papers

- [[2020 - Deep Metallic Surface Defect Detection The New Benchmark and Detection Network]] proposes EDDN and evaluates it on NEU-DET and GC10-DET.
