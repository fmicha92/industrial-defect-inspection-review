# CDO

## Definition

Collaborative Discrepancy Optimization is an unsupervised image anomaly localization method that optimizes normal and synthetic abnormal feature discrepancy distributions together. It targets overgeneralization in expert-apprentice feature-discrepancy models by enlarging the margin and reducing the overlap between normal and abnormal discrepancy distributions.

## Why It Matters

Unsupervised anomaly localization models can overgeneralize from normal training images and assign low anomaly scores to defective regions. CDO uses synthetic abnormal inputs from random perturbation to make the apprentice model separate normal and abnormal discrepancies more reliably without requiring real anomaly annotations during training.

## Used In These Papers

- [[2023 - Collaborative Discrepancy Optimization for Reliable Image Anomaly Localization]] proposes CDO and evaluates it on [[MVTec AD]], [[MVTec3D]], and an [[Automotive plastic parts dataset]].

## Method Details

- The margin optimization module minimizes normal feature discrepancies and maximizes synthetic abnormal feature discrepancies.
- The overlap optimization module dynamically weights hard normal and synthetic abnormal samples based on discrepancy-distribution statistics.
- Synthetic abnormal inputs are generated by random perturbation because real anomalies are unavailable during training.
- The implementation uses expert and apprentice feature extractors, including HRNet and ResNet-family backbones in the reported experiments.
