# Random Forest

## Definition

Random forest is an ensemble model that combines multiple randomized decision trees whose votes or averaged outputs form the prediction.

## Why It Matters

Random forests are strong tabular-data baselines and can provide feature-importance rankings for manufacturing datasets with many anonymized features.

## Used In These Papers

- [[2001 - Random Forests]] defines random forests, analyzes convergence and strength-correlation trade-offs, and introduces out-of-bag monitoring.
- [[2016 - Predict Failures in Production Lines A Two-stage Approach with Clustering and Supervised Learning]] uses random forest as the final cluster-specific classifier and for feature-importance-based feature selection.
- [[2025 - Comparison of ML classifiers in automatic diagnostics of PV panels using deep image features]] evaluates Random Forest and GridSearch Random Forest for PV panel defect classification.
