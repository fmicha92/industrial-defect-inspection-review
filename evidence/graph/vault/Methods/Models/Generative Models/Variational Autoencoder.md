# Variational Autoencoder

## Definition

A variational autoencoder is a generative model that learns a latent representation and reconstructs samples through a decoder. The linked review describes VAEs as optimizing an evidence lower bound and as generally more stable than GANs, while often producing blurrier images.

## Why It Matters

VAEs are a foundational learned generative synthesis method and provide a useful baseline when comparing image quality, diversity, controllability, and training stability across generative model families.
