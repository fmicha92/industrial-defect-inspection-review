# Sequence-to-Sequence Recurrent Network

## Definition

A sequence-to-sequence recurrent network maps an input sequence to an output sequence using recurrent encoder-decoder components such as SRNs, LSTMs, or GRUs.

## Why It Matters

Lake and Baroni show that this model family can perform nearly perfectly on random SCAN splits while failing on systematic compositional splits.

## Used In These Papers

- [[2018 - Generalization without Systematicity On the Compositional Skills of Sequence-to-Sequence Recurrent Networks]] evaluates SRN, LSTM, and GRU encoder-decoder models with and without attention.
