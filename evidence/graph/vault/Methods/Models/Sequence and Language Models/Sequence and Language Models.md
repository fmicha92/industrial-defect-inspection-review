# Sequence and Language Models

## Scope

Sequence and language models handle language, time series, logs, sensor streams, and multimodal reasoning over ordered data.

## Common Models

- RNNs, LSTMs, GRUs
- [[Transformers]]
- Encoder models: BERT-style
- Decoder models: GPT-style
- Encoder-decoder models: T5-style
- State-space models
