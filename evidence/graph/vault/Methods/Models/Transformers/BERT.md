# BERT

## Definition

BERT is a Transformer-encoder language representation model pretrained with masked language modeling and next sentence prediction, then fine-tuned with small task-specific output layers.

## Why It Matters

BERT demonstrated that deeply bidirectional Transformer pretraining could replace many task-specific NLP architectures across sentence-level and token-level tasks.

## Used In These Papers

- [[2019 - BERT Pre-training of Deep Bidirectional Transformers for Language Understanding]] introduces BERT and reports state-of-the-art results on GLUE, SQuAD, and SWAG.
