# Vision Transformer

## Definition

Vision Transformer is recorded as an architecture or method used by linked industrial defect inspection papers. Details not captured in the linked source text are `not reported`.

## Why It Matters

It provides a reusable model or generation mechanism for defect synthesis, classification, localization, or segmentation workflows.
