# Urban LabelMe

## Definition

Urban LabelMe is a real urban semantic-segmentation dataset used in the SYNTHIA experiments.

## Dataset Details

- Availability: not reported.
- Size reported in the SYNTHIA paper: 942 images, split into 200 training and 742 validation images.

## Related Papers

- [[2016 - The SYNTHIA Dataset A Large Collection of Synthetic Images for Semantic Segmentation of Urban Scenes]] evaluates SYNTHIA augmentation on Urban LabelMe.
