# PCB Defect Recognition Dataset

## Definition

The PCB defect recognition dataset is a real-world production dataset used by the Con-GAN paper to evaluate limited-data PCB defect recognition.

## Dataset Details

- The source reports five typical PCB defect classes: copper short, short, open, near open, and near short.
- Only 50 training samples per defect are available.
- The paper describes the defects as more irregular than NEU-CLS defects and harder to detect.
- Availability, license, and complete test-set size are not reported in the extracted source.

## Used In These Papers

- [[2023 - A New Contrastive GAN With Data Augmentation for Surface Defect Recognition Under Limited Data]] uses the dataset as a real-world PCB case and reports 91.84% accuracy with Con-GAN augmentation.
