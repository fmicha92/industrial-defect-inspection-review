# NYU hand pose dataset

## Definition

The NYU hand pose dataset contains depth frames for hand pose estimation.

## Dataset Details

- Reported size: 72,757 training frames and 8,251 testing frames.
- Sensor setup: three Kinect cameras, one frontal and two side views.
- Labels: hand pose information; SimGAN experiments train on 14 hand joints.
- Availability: not reported.

## Related Papers

- [[2017 - Learning from Simulated and Unsupervised Images through Adversarial Training]] evaluates hand pose estimation on real NYU hand pose test images after training on synthetic, refined synthetic, or real data.
