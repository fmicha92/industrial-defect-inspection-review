# OGD-DET

## Definition

OGD-DET is an optical-glass defect detection dataset built from defective optical glass images annotated by the IICT group. [[2022 - Video-Based Two-Stage Network for Optical Glass Sub-Millimeter Defect Detection]] reports that the images come from 40 optical-glass videos captured in a real industrial production environment.

## Why It Matters

OGD-DET supports [[Optical glass defect detection]] for industrial quality inspection where transparent or reflective glass materials can create controlled but visually challenging imaging conditions.

## Used In These Papers

- [[2022 - Video-Based Two-Stage Network for Optical Glass Sub-Millimeter Defect Detection]] introduces OGD-DET and evaluates video-based sub-millimeter defect detection.

## Dataset Details

- Domain: [[Glass inspection]].
- Task: [[Optical glass defect detection]].
- Images: 3415 total.
- Videos: 40 total; 25 for training and 15 for testing.
- Test images: 511.
- Source material: optical glass from the real factory of Innolight.
- Capture device: Omega industrial camera.
- Resolution: 1536 x 1024 pixels.
- Annotation status: images are annotated by the IICT group.
- Defect size: 0.1 mm to 0.53 mm, corresponding to 2 to 7 pixels in the reported image resolution.
- Annotation format: not reported.
- Defect categories: not reported.
- Access: dataset upon request; applicants email personal and organization information to zhouhan@iict.ac.cn, and eligible requests receive the data by email.
- License: Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International.
- Commercial use: not allowed.
