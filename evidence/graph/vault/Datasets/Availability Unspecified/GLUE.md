# GLUE

## Definition

GLUE is a benchmark collection of natural language understanding tasks.

## Dataset Details

- The BERT paper reports GLUE test results for MNLI, QQP, QNLI, SST-2, CoLA, STS-B, MRPC, and RTE.
- The BERT paper excludes WNLI from its average column.

## Used In These Papers

- [[2019 - BERT Pre-training of Deep Bidirectional Transformers for Language Understanding]] reports BERTBASE and BERTLARGE results on GLUE.
