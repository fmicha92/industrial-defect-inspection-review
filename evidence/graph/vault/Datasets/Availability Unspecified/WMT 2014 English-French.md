# WMT 2014 English-French

## Definition

WMT 2014 English-French is a machine-translation dataset used for English-to-French sequence transduction.

## Dataset Details

- Sentence pairs reported by the Transformer paper: 36 million.
- Tokenization reported by the Transformer paper: 32,000 word-piece vocabulary.
