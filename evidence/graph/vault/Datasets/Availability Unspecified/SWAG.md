# SWAG

## Definition

SWAG is a sentence-pair completion dataset for grounded commonsense inference.

## Dataset Details

- The BERT paper reports 113,000 sentence-pair completion examples.

## Used In These Papers

- [[2019 - BERT Pre-training of Deep Bidirectional Transformers for Language Understanding]] fine-tunes BERT on SWAG by scoring four candidate continuations.
