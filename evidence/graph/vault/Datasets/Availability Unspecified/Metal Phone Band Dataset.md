# Metal Phone Band Dataset

## Definition

The metal phone band dataset is reported as an evaluation dataset for controllable industrial defect synthesis. Availability, licensing, and complete collection details are not reported in the processed source note.

## Used In These Papers

- [[2022 - Mask-Guided Generation Method for Industrial Defect Images with Non-Uniform Structures]] uses phone-band production-line images for MDGAN synthesis, segmentation, and transfer experiments.
- [[2023 - Diversified and Multi-Class Controllable Industrial Defect Synthesis for Data Augmentation and Transfer]] evaluates DCDGANc synthetic augmentation on a metal phone band dataset; the paper crops extracted defect contents into 2,852 images of size 64 x 256 for generator training and evaluates segmentation at 128 x 128.
