# Woven fabric defect dataset

## Definition

Woven fabric defect dataset is a woven fabric anomaly detection dataset used by the GLASS paper to validate anomaly detection in an industrial textile setting. The paper reports 3,860 normal samples and 241 anomaly samples.

## Why It Matters

It supports evaluation or training for defect detection, localization, classification, segmentation, generation, or benchmark construction.

## Used In These Papers


- [[2024 - A Unified Anomaly Synthesis Strategy with Gradient Ascent for Industrial Anomaly Detection and Localization]] evaluates GLASS on this dataset and reports 100.0 image-level AUROC, 98.9 pixel-level AUROC, and 94.9 PRO for GLASS-j.
