# Synthetic turbocharger housing defect dataset

## Definition

This dataset is recorded in the processed Schmedemann et al. note as 4,906 synthetic images for turbocharger housing defect detection. Availability: `not reported`.

## Why It Matters

It anchors procedural generation, physically based rendering, and domain randomization for endoscopic industrial inspection.
