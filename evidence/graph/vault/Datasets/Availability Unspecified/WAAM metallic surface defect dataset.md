# WAAM Metallic Surface Defect Dataset

## Definition

WAAM metallic surface defect dataset represents the paper-specific wire-and-arc additive manufacturing surface-defect data in [[2023 - Deep Learning Based Online Metallic Surface Defect Detection Method for Wire and Arc Additive Manufacturing]]. The paper reports 760 tagged images and 8583 tagged targets.

## Why It Matters

It represents online metallic surface defect detection in additive manufacturing rather than conventional steel-sheet inspection.

## Used In These Papers

- [[2023 - Deep Learning Based Online Metallic Surface Defect Detection Method for Wire and Arc Additive Manufacturing]] uses WAAM surface imagery for online defect detection of welds, surface pores, grooves, and slag inclusions.
