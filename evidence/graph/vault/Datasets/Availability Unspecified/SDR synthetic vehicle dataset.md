# SDR synthetic vehicle dataset

## Definition

The SDR synthetic vehicle dataset is procedurally generated driving imagery created with structured domain randomization for vehicle detection.

## Dataset Details

- Generator: UE4 scene generator with structured context splines.
- Training size reported in the main comparison: 25K SDR images.
- Assets reported: 74 car models, 13 truck models, 5 bicycle models, 41 building models, 87 house models, 24 tree models, 20 pedestrian models, and 100 road sign models.
- Labels: rendered scene, depth, segmentation mask, 2D bounding boxes, 3D bounding boxes, truncation, and occlusion values.
- Availability: not reported.
