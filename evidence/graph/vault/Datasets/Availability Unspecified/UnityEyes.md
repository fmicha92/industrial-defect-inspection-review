# UnityEyes

## Definition

UnityEyes is a synthetic eye-image dataset used for gaze-estimation training in [[2017 - Learning from Simulated and Unsupervised Images through Adversarial Training]].

## Dataset Details

- Reported size: 1.2M synthetic images.
- Labels: gaze direction from the simulator.
- Availability: not reported.

## Related Papers

- [[2017 - Learning from Simulated and Unsupervised Images through Adversarial Training]] refines UnityEyes images with SimGAN and trains gaze estimators on the refined data.
