# SAPF

## Definition

SAPF is a solar aluminum profile frame surface defect dataset constructed from a real industrial scene. The source paper reports 150 defect images at 1280 x 256 resolution after manual cleaning.

## Why It Matters

It is used to test whether generated defect images improve object-detection performance in a limited-data industrial inspection setting.

## Used In These Papers

- [[2023 - A Data Generation Method with Dual Discriminators and Regularization for Surface Defect Detection Under Limited Data]] uses SAPF with a 100 train, 25 validation, and 25 test split for YOLO-v5 and YOLO-v8 defect detection experiments.
