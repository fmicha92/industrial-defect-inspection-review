# CamVid

## Definition

CamVid is a real urban semantic-segmentation dataset used in the SYNTHIA experiments.

## Dataset Details

- Availability: not reported.
- Size reported in the SYNTHIA paper: 701 images, split into 300 training and 401 validation images.

## Related Papers

- [[2016 - The SYNTHIA Dataset A Large Collection of Synthetic Images for Semantic Segmentation of Urban Scenes]] evaluates synthetic-plus-real semantic segmentation on CamVid.
