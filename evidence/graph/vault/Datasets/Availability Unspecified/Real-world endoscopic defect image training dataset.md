# Real-world endoscopic defect image training dataset

## Definition

This dataset is recorded in the processed Schmedemann et al. note as 110 real-world training images for turbocharger housing inspection. Availability: `not reported`.

## Why It Matters

It is the real-data fine-tuning counterpart to the synthetic turbocharger dataset.
