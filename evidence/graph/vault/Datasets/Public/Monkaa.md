# Monkaa

## Definition

Monkaa is a synthetic stereo-video dataset derived from an animated Blender scene, used for scene-flow, disparity, and optical-flow evaluation.

## Dataset Details

- Availability: freely available online according to the introducing paper.
- Size reported: 8,591 frames across 8 scenes at 960x540.
- Labels: optical flow, disparity/depth, disparity change, object/material segmentation, 3D positions, camera parameters, and motion boundaries.
- Visual setting: nonrigid and articulated motion, including soft body motion and fur.

## Related Papers

- [[2016 - A Large Dataset to Train Convolutional Networks for Disparity Optical Flow and Scene Flow Estimation]] introduces Monkaa as one component of the synthetic scene-flow suite.
