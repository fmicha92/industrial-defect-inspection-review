# Driving Synthetic Scene Flow Dataset

## Definition

The Driving synthetic scene-flow dataset is a KITTI-like rendered stereo driving sequence for disparity, optical flow, and scene-flow experiments.

## Dataset Details

- Availability: freely available online according to the introducing paper.
- Size reported: 4,392 frames, one scene, 960x540 resolution.
- Labels: optical flow, disparity/depth, disparity change, segmentation, 3D positions, camera parameters, and motion boundaries.
- Scene design: road scene with car models, trees, street lights, and a stereo baseline intended to resemble KITTI geometry.
