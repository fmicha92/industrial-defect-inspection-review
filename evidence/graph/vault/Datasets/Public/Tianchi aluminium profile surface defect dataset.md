# Tianchi aluminium profile surface defect dataset

## Definition

The Tianchi aluminium profile surface defect dataset is used for workpiece surface defect detector generalization in the YOLOv11 edge-computing paper. The paper reports four defect types: bruise, dirty, raised, and scratches.

## Why It Matters

It tests whether YOLOv11 with SA-GAN augmentation generalizes beyond the NEU-DET steel-strip setting to aluminium profile surface defects.

## Used In These Papers

- [[2025 - Workpiece Surface Defect Detection Based on YOLOv11 and Edge Computing]] augments the dataset with SA-GAN to 872 defect images and reports YOLOv11 with SA-GAN at 87.0% mAP@0.5.

## Source Anchors

- The paper reports an SA-GAN-augmented set of 872 images: 208 bruise, 188 dirty, 168 raised, and 308 scratch images.
