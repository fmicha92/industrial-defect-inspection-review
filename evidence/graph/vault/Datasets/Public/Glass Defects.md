# Glass Defects

## Definition

Glass Defects is a public Kaggle object-detection dataset for laminated glass defect detection. It contains high-resolution images of small laminated glass samples with manually labeled defect instances.

## Why It Matters

Glass Defects supports [[Laminated glass defect detection]] with bounding-box annotations in YOLO format. It adds a laminated-glass inspection source to the glass domain, complementing aircraft canopy, smartphone screen glass, optical glass, and glass-container datasets already represented in the graph.
