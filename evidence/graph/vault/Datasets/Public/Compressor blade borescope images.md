# Compressor blade borescope images

## Definition

Compressor blade borescope images are used in the processed aircraft engine defect detection note as inspection-video frames for blade defect segmentation. The source paper states that the data are publicly available from the RB211 535 HPC4 borescope inspection video by RVI Ltd. Remote Visual Inspections on YouTube.

## Why It Matters

They connect aircraft maintenance, borescope inspection, deblurring, and U-Net segmentation.

## Used In These Papers

- [[2023 - A Deep-Learning-Based Approach for Aircraft Engine Defect Detection]] uses this dataset as reported in the processed paper note.
