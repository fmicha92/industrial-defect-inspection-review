# SYNTHIA

## Definition

SYNTHIA is a synthetic urban-driving dataset for semantic segmentation, generated in a Unity virtual city.

## Dataset Details

- Availability: the introducing paper reports the dataset is available at `adas.cvc.uab.es/synthia`.
- Classes: sky, building, road, sidewalk, fence, vegetation, lane-marking, pole, car, traffic signs, pedestrians, cyclists, and miscellaneous.
- SYNTHIA-Rand: 13,400 randomized frames at 960x720.
- SYNTHIA-Seqs: four seasonal sequences of about 50,000 frames each.
- Additional data: depth maps and multiple viewpoints are reported.

## Related Papers

- [[2016 - The SYNTHIA Dataset A Large Collection of Synthetic Images for Semantic Segmentation of Urban Scenes]] introduces SYNTHIA and evaluates its use for semantic segmentation transfer to real urban datasets.
