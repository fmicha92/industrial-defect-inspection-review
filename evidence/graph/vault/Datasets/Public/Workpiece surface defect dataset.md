# Workpiece surface defect dataset

## Definition

Workpiece surface defect data used by the YOLOv11 edge-computing paper for model training, validation, and evaluation. The paper's data availability statement reports the GitCode URL and says the relevant data can be used without restriction for reproducibility.

## Why It Matters

This item supports industrial defect detection, anomaly detection, classification, segmentation, generation, or benchmark evaluation.

## Used In These Papers

- [[2025 - Workpiece Surface Defect Detection Based on YOLOv11 and Edge Computing]] uses the data host together with NEU-DET and Tianchi surface-defect experiments for YOLOv11 and YOLOv11-RKNN evaluation.
