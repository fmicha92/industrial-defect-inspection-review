# SCAN Benchmark

## Definition

The SCAN benchmark tests whether sequence-to-sequence models can map simplified commands to action sequences under systematic generalization splits.

## Benchmark Details

- Dataset: [[SCAN]]
- Primary task: [[Compositional generalization]]
- Metric: [[Exact-match accuracy]]
