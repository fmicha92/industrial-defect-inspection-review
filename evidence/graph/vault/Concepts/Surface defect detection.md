# Surface defect detection

## Definition

Surface defect detection is the automated identification of visible defects on manufactured surfaces. It appears in image-based industrial inspection settings ranging from turbocharger housing surfaces to textured industrial materials, aircraft engine blades, construction defects, and fiber placement inspection.

## Why It Matters

The papers treat surface defect detection as a practical bottleneck for industrial quality control: real defects are rare, annotation is expensive, defect classes can be imbalanced, and models often need synthetic data, simulation, or unsupervised anomaly detection to work with limited real examples.
