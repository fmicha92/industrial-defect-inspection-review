# Perovskite Solar Cells

## Definition

Perovskite solar cells are photovoltaic devices with a perovskite-structured absorber layer in a multilayer stack that includes electron and hole transport layers.

## Why It Matters

The linked paper studies lead-free MASnI3 absorber layers as an alternative to lead-based perovskites and evaluates how transport materials, contact metals, absorber defects, dopant concentration, and temperature affect device performance.
