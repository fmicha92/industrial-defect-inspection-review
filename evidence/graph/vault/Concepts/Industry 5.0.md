# Industry 5.0

## Definition

Industry 5.0 frames industrial automation as a human-centric collaboration between human expertise and intelligent automation. In [[2025 - AI-driven fabric classification]], the term is used to motivate real-time textile quality control that combines AI-based fabric classification with human-centered production goals.

## Why It Matters

For visual inspection systems, Industry 5.0 emphasizes automation that improves efficiency while keeping industrial work, sustainability, and resource use in view.
