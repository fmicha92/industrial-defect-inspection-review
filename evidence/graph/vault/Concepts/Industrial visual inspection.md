# Industrial visual inspection

## Definition

Industrial visual inspection uses imaging systems and automated analysis to detect defects or quality issues in manufactured parts. The paper notes cover RGB/endoscopic images, laser line scan depth images, borescope images, textured surface images, and CT volumes.

## Why It Matters

The papers frame visual inspection as a production problem where defective samples are scarce, annotation is costly, and inspection speed or deployment constraints matter. This makes synthetic data, anomaly detection, segmentation, and edge deployment recurring technical themes.
