# Metallic Surface Defect Detection

## Definition

Metallic surface defect detection locates and classifies defects on metal surfaces such as steel sheets or hot-rolled steel strips.

## Why It Matters

The task appears in the vault as an industrial visual inspection problem where illumination, reflection, material variation, defect scale, and data imbalance make deployment difficult.

## Used In These Papers

- [[2013 - A Noise Robust Method Based on Completed Local Binary Patterns for Hot-Rolled Steel Strip Surface Defects]] describes acquisition, rapid detection, feature extraction, and classification stages for hot-rolled steel strip surface defects and introduces [[NEU-CLS]].
- [[2020 - PGA-Net Pyramid Feature Fusion and Global Context Attention Network for Automated Surface Defect Detection]] introduces [[NEU-SEG]] for pixel-wise hot-rolled steel strip surface defect segmentation.
- [[2020 - An End-to-End Steel Surface Defect Detection Approach via Fusing Multiple Hierarchical Features]] introduces [[NEU-DET]] and [[Defect Detection Network]] for hot-rolled steel strip surface defect detection.
- [[2022 - MSFT-YOLO Improved YOLOv5 Based on Transformer for Detecting Defects of Steel Surface]] reports 0.752 mAP and 30.6 FPS on NEU-DET.
- [[2020 - Surface Defect Saliency of Magnetic Tile]] introduces [[Magnetic Tile Defects]] and evaluates [[MCuePush U-Net]] for magnetic-tile surface-defect saliency and localization.
- [[2025 - You Only Look Once-Aluminum]] evaluates [[YOLO-AL]] on [[APDDD]] for aluminum profile surface defect detection.
- [[2023 - IDD-Net Industrial Defect Detection Method Based on Deep-Learning]] evaluates [[IDD-Net]] on NEU steel defects and the [[Baidu aluminium surface defect dataset]].


- [[2021 - Deep Learning-Based Defect Detection of Metal Parts Evaluating Current Methods in Complex Conditions]] supports metal part defect detection, complex industrial conditions.
- [[2020 - Deep Metallic Surface Defect Detection The New Benchmark and Detection Network]] introduces GC10-DET and evaluates EDDN for metallic surface defect detection.
- [[2021 - X-SDD A New Benchmark for Hot Rolled Steel Strip Surface Defects Detection]] introduces [[X-SDD]] for hot-rolled steel strip surface defect classification and benchmarks CNN classifiers.
- [[2023 - Deep Learning Based Online Metallic Surface Defect Detection Method for Wire and Arc Additive Manufacturing]] detects WAAM welds, surface pores, grooves, and slag inclusions with [[YOLO-attention]].
- [[2025 - CSDD A Benchmark Dataset for Casting Surface Defect Detection and Segmentation]] is a related casting-specific metal surface benchmark with detection and segmentation annotations.
- [[Metal Surface Defects Dataset]] is a Kaggle package derived from [[GC10-DET]] rather than a standalone metal surface defect dataset.
