# Pharmaceutical Bag Sealing Defect Detection

## Definition

Pharmaceutical bag sealing defect detection identifies leakage-prone defects in heat-sealed plastic pharmaceutical bags.

## Why It Matters

Small defects in liquid-drug packaging can leak slowly and may only become apparent during transportation. Automated nondestructive inspection is intended to reduce missed defects and support full inspection rather than manual sampling.
