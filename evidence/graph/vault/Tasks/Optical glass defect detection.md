# Optical Glass Defect Detection

## Definition

Optical glass defect detection identifies defects in optical-glass imagery collected from industrial inspection settings. [[2022 - Video-Based Two-Stage Network for Optical Glass Sub-Millimeter Defect Detection]] frames the task around sub-millimeter defects on high-resolution optical-glass videos.

## Why It Matters

Optical glass inspection supports quality control for transparent glass components where production defects need to be detected from high-resolution industrial imagery.

## Used In These Datasets

- [[OGD-DET]] contains 3415 annotated optical-glass images from a controlled real-world industrial environment.

## Used In These Papers

- [[2022 - Video-Based Two-Stage Network for Optical Glass Sub-Millimeter Defect Detection]] proposes a video-based two-stage detector for this task.
