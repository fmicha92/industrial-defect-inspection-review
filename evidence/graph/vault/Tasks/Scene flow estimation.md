# Scene Flow Estimation

## Definition

Scene flow estimation predicts dense 3D motion in a scene, commonly from stereo video or related multi-view inputs.

- [[2016 - A Large Dataset to Train Convolutional Networks for Disparity Optical Flow and Scene Flow Estimation]] provides synthetic ground truth for scene-flow estimation and evaluates SceneFlowNet.
