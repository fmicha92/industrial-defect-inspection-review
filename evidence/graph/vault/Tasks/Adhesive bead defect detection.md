# Adhesive Bead Defect Detection

## Definition

Adhesive bead defect detection identifies quality problems in applied structural adhesive before bonded automotive parts proceed further through production. [[2021 - Simulation-Based Data Augmentation for Quality Inspection of Structural Adhesive]] and [[2021 - Generative Adversarial Networks for Data Augmentation in Structural Adhesive Inspection]] focus on discontinuities and excess adhesive in bead images.

## Why It Matters

Structural adhesive defects can be costly or impossible to correct after bonding. Detecting discontinuities and excess material before or during assembly supports continuous inspection beyond destructive sampling.

## Used In These Papers

- [[2021 - Simulation-Based Data Augmentation for Quality Inspection of Structural Adhesive]] trains YOLOv4-family detectors on real and simulation-generated adhesive bead images.
- [[2021 - Generative Adversarial Networks for Data Augmentation in Structural Adhesive Inspection]] trains StyleGAN2-ADA and YOLOv4-Tiny for GAN-augmented adhesive bead defect detection.
