# Defect detection

## Definition

Defect detection identifies whether and where industrial products contain defects. In this vault it includes object-detection, segmentation, and anomaly-detection formulations when the paper reports detection-oriented outputs.

## Used In These Papers

- [[2023 - A Data Generation Method with Dual Discriminators and Regularization for Surface Defect Detection Under Limited Data]] evaluates generated surface defect images by training YOLO-v5 and YOLO-v8 defect detectors on SAPF and NEU-CLS.
- [[2026 - Texture Conditioned GAN Based Self Supervised Framework for Fabric Defect Detection]] treats fabric defect detection as normal-only anomaly detection with image-level anomaly scores and pixel-level heatmaps.
