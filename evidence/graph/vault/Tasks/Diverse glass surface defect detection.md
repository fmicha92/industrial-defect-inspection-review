# Diverse Glass Surface Defect Detection

## Definition

Diverse glass surface defect detection localizes visible defects across multiple glass product contexts. The GSDNet repository groups smartphone screen glass, aircraft glass, automotive glass, recycled mobile screen glass, and construction glass datasets under this task family.

## Why It Matters

Glass surface inspection varies across product geometry, transparency, lighting, and defect scale. Grouping these datasets supports comparison of methods that claim robustness across multiple glass-surface inspection contexts.

## Used In These Datasets

- [[SSGD]] covers smartphone screen glass defects.
- [[AGDD]] covers aircraft glass canopy defects.
- [[AuGD]] covers automotive glass defects.
- [[RSGD]] covers recycled mobile screen defects.
- [[CGD]] covers construction glass defects.

## Used In These Papers

- [[2024 - Defect Detection in Automotive Glass Based on Modified YOLOv5]] evaluates automotive-glass defect detection on [[AuGD]].
