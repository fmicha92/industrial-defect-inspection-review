# Manufacturing Failure Prediction

## Definition

Manufacturing failure prediction is the task of predicting whether a part, component, machine, or production step will fail a quality-control or operational criterion from process measurements collected before or during production.

## Why It Matters

Failure prediction can support earlier intervention in manufacturing lines, reduce quality losses, and prioritize inspection or rework before defective parts move further through production.
