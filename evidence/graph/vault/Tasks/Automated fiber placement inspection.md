# Automated fiber placement inspection

## Definition

Automated fiber placement inspection checks composite manufacturing defects in fiber layup processes. In the processed note, the inspection data are grayscale depth images from a laser line scan sensor.

## Why It Matters

The local paper frames AFP defect inspection as a small-data problem: defect classes have only 22 to 166 images, motivating synthetic image generation with DCGAN and geometric transformations.
