# Casting Surface Defect Detection

## Definition

Casting surface defect detection locates and classifies defects on casting or casting-like metal surfaces. In the processed CSDD paper, the target defects are scratches, spots, and rusts on structured metal plates designed to simulate casting surfaces.

## Why It Matters

The task is difficult because casting-like surface structures, reflective metal imaging, small defects, and defect shape variation can make defect regions hard to distinguish from background structure.

## Used In These Papers

- [[2025 - CSDD A Benchmark Dataset for Casting Surface Defect Detection and Segmentation]] introduces [[CSDD]] and benchmarks detection and segmentation methods for casting surface defects.
- [[2025 - A Dataset for Surface Defect Detection on Complex Structured Parts Based on Photometric Stereo]] introduces [[MSDD]] for detecting eight defect types on non-planar casting metal parts using photometric-stereo-derived pseudo-color images.
