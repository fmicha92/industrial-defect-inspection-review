# Scientific Image Generation

## Definition

Scientific image generation synthesizes, edits, varies, or inpaints images from scientific instruments or scientific visual domains while preserving scientifically meaningful structure.

## Why It Matters

Generated scientific images can support data augmentation, simulation, interpolation, visualization, and hypothesis generation when real images are scarce or costly. The main risk is that visually plausible outputs may be scientifically wrong.
