# Car Damage Detection

## Definition

Car damage detection locates and classifies visible damage on vehicle images, including damage categories such as dents, scratches, cracks, glass shatter, tire flat, and broken lamps.

## Why It Matters

It supports automated damage assessment in insurance and vehicle inspection settings where image-level classification alone is not enough to identify damage location or extent.
