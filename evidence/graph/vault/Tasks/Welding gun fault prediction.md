# Welding Gun Fault Prediction

## Definition

Welding gun fault prediction is the task of using historical welding-gun sensor and control time series to anticipate future welding-gun faults or welding parameters.

## Why It Matters

Faults in resistance spot welding guns can stop automotive manufacturing lines and cause unplanned downtime. Predicting these faults supports maintenance planning and more reliable body-shop production.

## Used In These Datasets

- [[RSW gun fault prediction benchmark dataset]] provides multivariate time series for forecasting welding parameters and predicting resistance spot welding gun faults.

## Used In These Papers

- [[2024 - Benchmark for Welding Gun Fault Prediction with Multivariate Time Series Data]] defines the task as forecasting welding-gun target parameters and mapping the forecast horizon to normal or fault states.
