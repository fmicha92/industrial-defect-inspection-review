# PCB Defect Classification

## Definition

PCB defect classification assigns located PCB defect regions to defect categories such as missing hole, mouse bite, open circuit, short, spur, and spurious copper when those labels are available.

## Why It Matters

Classification turns localization output into actionable manufacturing categories for PCB quality control.

- [[2020 - HRIPCB a Challenging Dataset for PCB Defects Detection and Classification]] trains an end-to-end CNN classifier after reference-based defect localization.
