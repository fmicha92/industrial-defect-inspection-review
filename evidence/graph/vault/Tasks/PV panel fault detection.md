# PV Panel Fault Detection

## Definition

PV panel fault detection localizes or classifies faults and damage in photovoltaic panels, often from infrared, thermal, RGB, or electroluminescence imagery.

## Why It Matters

Faults such as hotspots, cracks, delamination, dust coverage, and degraded modules can reduce solar-farm energy yield. Automated detection supports scalable inspection and maintenance for large PV installations.
