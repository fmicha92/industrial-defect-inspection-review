# Compositional Generalization

## Definition

Compositional generalization is the ability to recombine learned parts or rules into new structured combinations.

## Used In These Papers

- [[2018 - Generalization without Systematicity On the Compositional Skills of Sequence-to-Sequence Recurrent Networks]] evaluates recurrent sequence-to-sequence models on SCAN splits that require systematic recombination.
