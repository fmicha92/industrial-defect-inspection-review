# Semiconductor Wafer Dicing Fault Classification

## Definition

Semiconductor wafer dicing fault classification assigns chip or dicing-street imagery to flawless or faulty classes based on whether the wafer separation path deviates from the intended scribe area toward the chip interior.

## Why It Matters

Dicing faults affect yield after wafer separation. The task is difficult when faults are micrometer-scale, rare, and visible only as small deviations in high-resolution wafer imagery.
