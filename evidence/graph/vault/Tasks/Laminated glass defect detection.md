# Laminated Glass Defect Detection

## Definition

Laminated glass defect detection localizes visible manufacturing defects in laminated glass samples. [[Glass Defects]] frames the task around manually labeled bounding boxes for defects such as bubbles, delamination, and metallic particles.

## Why It Matters

Laminated glass inspection supports quality control for transparent layered glass products where internal or surface defects can affect appearance and reliability.

## Used In These Datasets

- [[Glass Defects]] provides YOLO-format bounding-box annotations for laminated glass defect instances.
