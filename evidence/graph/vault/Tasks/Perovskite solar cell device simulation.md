# Perovskite Solar Cell Device Simulation

## Definition

Perovskite solar cell device simulation uses numerical device models to evaluate photovoltaic layer stacks, transport layers, absorber properties, contacts, and operating conditions before fabrication.

## Why It Matters

Simulation can narrow the design space for lead-free or low-cost photovoltaic devices by estimating performance metrics before experimental fabrication.
