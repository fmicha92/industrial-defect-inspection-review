# Urban Semantic Segmentation

## Definition

Urban semantic segmentation assigns semantic class labels to pixels in road-scene imagery.

## Used In These Papers

- [[2016 - The SYNTHIA Dataset A Large Collection of Synthetic Images for Semantic Segmentation of Urban Scenes]] uses SYNTHIA to improve segmentation on CamVid, KITTI, Urban LabelMe, and CBCL StreetScenes.
- [[2022 - SolarDK A high-resolution urban solar panel image classification and localization dataset]] treats PV-system localization in Danish aerial imagery as a solar-specific urban segmentation task.
