# Solar Panel Localization

## Definition

Solar panel localization identifies the pixels or regions in an aerial image that correspond to solar photovoltaic systems.

## Why It Matters

Pixel-level PV localization supports mapping installed solar capacity and spatial adoption patterns more precisely than image-level classification alone.
