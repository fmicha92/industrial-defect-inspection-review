# Glass Bangle Defect Classification

## Definition

Glass bangle defect classification assigns factory-collected glass bangle images to quality classes such as broken, defected, or good. [[Glass Bangle Defect Detection Classification]] frames the task as a three-class image classification problem.

## Why It Matters

Glass bangle manufacturing requires rejecting broken or visibly defective products before shipment. Image classification supports low-cost visual quality screening for a glass product category that is distinct from flat glass, optical glass, containers, and screen glass.

## Used In These Datasets

- [[Glass Bangle Defect Detection Classification]] contains 1080 human-labeled factory images grouped into broken, defected, and good classes.
